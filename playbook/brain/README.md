# The True Economy brain (self-improving analytics)
Goal: learn from Buffer metrics which series, caption styles and platform pairings work, recommend changes, and only act on big changes after the user approves. Everything is backed by data in this folder.

Files: posts.jsonl (every sent post: id, platform, series, caption features) | metrics.jsonl (append-only metric snapshots) | config.json (thresholds and what needs approval) | scorecard.md (latest analysis) | insights.md (plain-English weekly digest) | guidance.md (soft caption guidance the posting tasks read) | decisions.jsonl (pending / approved / declined big changes) | routing.json (series x platform exclusions, applied by the posting tasks) | series_overrides.json (manual backfill of series for posts before the logs existed).
Scripts: scripts/brain_ingest.py, brain_analyze.py, brain_decide.py.

Weekly (Sunday evening UK): ingest all sent posts with metrics -> analyse -> rewrite insights.md and the AUTO block of guidance.md -> queue BIG proposals -> message the user. Metrics count only after maturity_days (4).
Rules: index = value / that platform's own median; a verdict needs n >= 3 matured posts for a series on a platform, 75% of them on the same side of the median and (TikTok) watch time agreeing; a BIG proposal needs n >= 4 and an effect of at least 40%. Caption rules need >= 10 posts on each side. Otherwise: "not enough data".
Big changes (need user approval, applied only by brain_decide.py approve): dropping/pausing/replacing a series, adding a series or changing rotation, stopping a series on a platform, changing posting times or queue mode, caption rules beyond soft guidance. The brain never touches template, colours, verification standards or hashtag limits.
New series idea: run `python3 scripts/brain_analyze.py --rank`, pick the weakest eligible series (n >= 3 with a verdict) that shares features with the idea's weakness, propose it as the replacement, and give the evidence. If no series has enough data yet, say so and suggest a 4-post trial in the exploration slot (20% of posts).
Platform routing: when a series is approved as WEAK on a platform, routing.json lists it and the posting tasks skip that platform for that series, freeing queue slots (Buffer free plan: 10 scheduled posts per channel).

## Series ranking (added 23 Nov 2026, user-designed; scripts/brain_rank.py)
Goal: post more of what works, less of what doesn't, give every series a fair test, and tell the user which series to consider scrapping.
- Weekly, after ingest + analyse: `python3 scripts/brain_rank.py` writes ranking.json (slots per series, read by scripts/anim_plan.py and scripts/daily_plan.py), ranking.md (paste into the weekly report), ranking_history.jsonl, rank_proposals.json (queue with `brain_decide.py add playbook/brain/rank_proposals.json`).
- Two pools, each with its own league table: animated (Animated Shorts) and daily (Daily Post). Markets Daily / Stock Market Today / clips are not ranked.
- Score 50 = typical for that pool. typical result (floor) + watch time + hit bonus (ceiling; full for 28 days, then halves weekly). A viral hit is a real signal, not noise: it lifts the series at once and protects it from the scrapping list while recent.
- Groups: new (< 6 matured videos: priority slots, score pulled towards "unknown, not bad") / top / middle / bottom / rested.
- Slots are SHARES of the window, so the same rules hold for any number of series or posts per day: about 30% for testing (new + bottom), 70% for top + middle by score, nobody above 43% of the window. More series than a week holds -> the window grows to 14/21/28 days and low series take turns.
- To post more often change one number (animated: registry.json limits.max_posts_per_day; daily: config.json rank.pools.daily.posts_per_day) and give the new slot a posting time.
- AUTOMATIC: slot changes between series, ONLY when the data check passes (latest Buffer snapshot within 36 h, all three platforms, >= 90% of logged posts from the last 21 days have numbers). If the check fails the old slots stay and a `rank:confirm:<pool>:<week>` decision asks the user.
- ALWAYS NEEDS THE USER'S YES: resting a series (bench: design and ideas kept), scrapping, a relaunch (second chance: one fix, judged on 3 new videos), adding a series. Flagged when a series is in the bottom group 3 weekly runs in a row with >= 6 videos and no recent hit, or when a pool has more live series than it can test. Apply with brain_decide.py approve / unrest.
- config.json rank.enabled = false keeps both daily tasks on their old rules; true switches the ranking on.
- Ingest fix (23 Nov): sent TikTok videos lose our file name on Buffer, so they are matched to their Instagram/Facebook twin by thumbnail id (then by the 17:00 animated log); "h2h" (digit) now maps; the Daily Post log is used only for posts in the 19:30 slot. Before this, animated TikToks were counted as that day's Daily Post series.
